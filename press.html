The small choices behind the pictures

Making the kennel.

Each character gets a silhouette.
Each cricket role gets its equipment.

01

A look of their own.

Brian Labra’s revised design uses short, clean-cut fur around his head and neck. Chris Tail keeps his distinct locks. Breed, ears, muzzle and posture give each original dog a recognisable silhouette.

02

Spin starts on the left.

Monty Spanesar is a black-and-white English springer spaniel with feathered floppy ears below a neatly wrapped navy turban. His left-arm bowling study brings cricket mechanics into the character design.

03

You can read the role.

A batter’s willow, a bowler’s ball and a wicketkeeper’s gloves help the role read at a glance. Action illustrations explore the right equipment and pose for each cricket moment.

04

Tea means an interval.

Brian’s tea scene includes a visible drink and something to eat, with his bat resting nearby. The pavilion becomes a place for company between overs.
