Install your desktop / Codex terminal pet＋
  1. Unzip your teammate. Each ZIP contains a named pet folder with its manifest, animation and instructions.
  2. Put it in your pet folder. Copy that folder into ~/.codex/pets/. Back up an existing folder with the same name before replacing it.
  3. Choose your pet. In the desktop app, open Settings → Pets → Refresh. In an interactive Codex terminal, type /pets and choose the dog.

Terminal pets need iTerm2 3.6 or later, or Kitty/Sixel support. These extended desktop packages are separate from ChatGPT web’s sprite upload format. Official pet guide ↗

Keep a good dog close. Download the artwork, share a reaction, or turn a favourite into a sticker.

Bring a teammate into WhatsApp＋
  1. Download and unpack. Save the sticker ZIP, then open it to extract the individual image files.
  2. Keep your favourites. Save the images you want to your device’s photos or files.
  3. Create your sticker. Open a chat in WhatsApp and use its Create sticker option. Choose a saved image, adjust it, then save or send it.

The ZIP is a collection of images; downloading it does not automatically install a WhatsApp sticker pack. WhatsApp’s sticker guide ↗
